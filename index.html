Settings

Most WoW Forever addons are published on CurseForge. It only lets tools like this search and download with a free personal key.

  1. Open console.curseforge.com and sign in.
  2. Open “API keys” in the left menu and copy the key shown there.
  3. Paste it below and save. It stays on this computer.

Install from a file you downloaded

Works without any key: download an addon’s .zip in your browser, then pick it here. If the addon is already on your shelf it is updated in place.
